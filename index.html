a silly fact first

The = sign exists because a man got bored.

1557. A Welsh guy called Robert Recorde got sick of writing "is equalle to" over and over.

So he drew two little lines, because "noe .2. thynges can be moare equalle."

That's it. That's the whole reason. Most of maths is people being lazy and then dressing it up after.
