a silly fact first

The = sign exists because a man got bored.

1557. A Welsh guy called Robert Recorde got sick of writing "is equalle to" over and over.

a small betrayal
Something your lecturer will probably not tell you.
A lot of why maths looks frightening is that it was named by people who hated writing things out in full.
sin is short for sine, which came from a mistranslation of an Arabic word, which came from a Sanskrit word for a bowstring. Nobody ever meant for it to be called that. It is a typo that lasted a thousand years.
So when the notation feels arbitrary and a bit unfair: that is because a fair amount of it genuinely is.

These three are mostly bookkeeping.
