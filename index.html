a silly fact first

The = sign exists because a man got bored.

1557. A Welsh mathematician called Robert Recorde was sick of writing "is equalle to" over and over again.

So he drew two little lines, because "noe .2. thynges can be moare equalle."

That's it. That's the entire reason. Most of maths is people being lazy and then making it look fancy afterwards.
